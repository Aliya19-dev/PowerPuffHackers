# Literature Review & Market Research

Several research papers, institutional case studies, and academic analyses examine agricultural disintermediation and direct-to-consumer agri-tech platforms in India:

* **[AgriConnect: A Smart Agricultural Marketplace Application for Direct Farmer-to-Customer Selling](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/404899542_AGRICONNECT_A_Smart_Agricultural_Marketplace_Application_for_Direct_Farmer-to-_Customer_Selling)** (ResearchGate, 2026)  
  Examines the implementation of direct mobile platforms for product listing, order management, and real-time communication to reduce farmer dependency on traditional middlemen.

* **[The Indian Agriculture Industry Supply Chain Management: A Critical Study](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/352705609_THE_INDIAN_AGRICULTURE_INDUSTRY_SUPPLY_CHAIN_MANAGEMENT_A_CRITICAL_STUDY)** (ResearchGate)  
  Highlights structural inefficiencies in traditional Indian agri-supply chains, showing that farmers often receive only 30–35% of the final consumer market price due to multi-layered intermediary exploitation.

* **[IFFCO Digital Farmer Marketplace Platform Case Study](https://www.mobiloitte.com/casestudy/iffco-digital-farmer-marketplace-platform-empowering-indian-agriculture)**  
  Details the design and real-world deployment of a unified digital agri-marketplace in India, focusing on direct buyer-seller transactions, transparent input pricing, and multilingual accessibility.

* **[Food Supply Chain Management in Indian Agriculture](https://mpra.ub.uni-muenchen.de/60441/1/MPRA_paper_60441.pdf)** (Munich Personal RePEc Archive)  
  Analyzes supply chain bottlenecks across Indian agricultural subsystems and emphasizes the role of Information and Communication Technologies (ICTs) in streamlining market access.

* **[National Agriculture Market (eNAM) Official Portal](https://enam.gov.in/)**  
  The pan-India electronic trading portal networking APMC mandis to create a unified national market, serving as a primary benchmark for digital price discovery and online produce trading in India.
